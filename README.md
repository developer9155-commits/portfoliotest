# Murari Kumar — 3D Cinematic Developer Portfolio & Miniature City 🌆✨

A premium, interactive 3D developer portfolio website built for **Murari Kumar**, Diploma Student in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Web Developer, and AI Product Builder.

Features an expanded miniature 3D city with the **Astropulse SMM Corporate Campus** (with an automated security barrier gate and 8 animated employees), suburban residential houses with daily commuters, an entertaining cycling commuter who tumbles/falls and gets back up, an active farmer hoeing at the **CropIndia AI** terraced fields, a welcoming guide gesturing with both hands, the futuristic **Nexora AI** crystal station, mountain windmill, camper van campsite, drifting clouds, and native Web Audio atmosphere.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Pure Static Architecture (No Build Tools Required)**
   - No Node.js, npm, Vite, webpack, or terminal commands needed.
   - Works straight out of the box in any modern browser.
   - 100% compatible with free **GitHub Pages** hosting.

2. **Expanded 3D Miniature City (Three.js)**
   - Scaled-up floating city island with asphalt avenues, center line striping, and pedestrian zebra crossings.
   - **Astropulse SMM Corporate Campus:** Modern multi-story glass-and-steel tower with rooftop neon signage and an **automated motorized security barrier gate** that opens and closes on a smooth periodic loop.
   - **8 Animated Employees with Assigned Roles:**
     - 👮 **Gate Security Guard:** Salutes visitors and operates gate controls.
     - 💻 **Server & Database Engineer:** Monitors blinking server racks and types at the terminal.
     - 📊 **Social Media Growth Strategist:** Gestures at a glowing holographic analytics board.
     - 🎧 **Customer Support Executive:** Wears a headset, nodding and answering inquiries.
     - 📦 **Courier & Dispatcher:** Carries delivery package boxes between office and gate.
     - ☕ **Coffee Break Teammate:** Holds a coffee mug on the terrace and takes sips.
     - 🎨 **Creative UI/UX Designer:** Works at open-plan desk with dual screens.
     - ⚡ **API Automation Engineer:** Monitors high-throughput webhook feeds.
   - **Residential District & Commuters:**
     - Cozy suburban houses with pitched roofs and front doors.
     - **Walking Commuter:** Leaves their house doorway, walks down the sidewalk to the city center, works, walks back home, and enters through the door!
   - **The Falling Cyclist:**
     - Cycles down the main avenue, hits a road bump, wobbles, tumbles/falls down to the asphalt, sits up, shakes off the dust, lifts the bike upright, and pedals off again!
   - **Farming at CropIndia AI:**
     - Active farmer character wearing a straw hat swinging a farming hoe into the soil on a rhythmic cycle.
   - **Dual-Hand Gesturing Welcome Guide:**
     - Low-poly avatar representing Murari Kumar at the About Plaza actively moving and gesturing with both arms to welcome visitors.
   - **Camper Van & Campsite:** Retro coral camper van, campfire with flickering point light, surfboard, and mountain windmill.
   - **Nexora AI Tech Station:** Floating glowing cyan octahedron energy crystal and orbital communications ring.

3. **Featured Projects**
   - 📈 **Astropulse SMM:** Automated social media marketing & services provider platform (`https://astropulsesmm.vercel.app/`).
   - 🚀 **Nexora AI:** AI-powered website builder platform (`https://nexora-ai-webbuilder.vercel.app/`).
   - 🌾 **CropIndia AI:** Agricultural intelligence and crop production analytics concept.

4. **Cinematic Camera Transitions (GSAP)**
   - Smooth dual-coordinate camera position & target interpolation.
   - 1-click navigation to:
     - 🌐 **City Overview**
     - 👤 **About Me (Guide Plaza)**
     - ⚡ **Technical Skills (Windmill Ridge)**
     - 📈 **Astropulse SMM (Corporate HQ & Gate)**
     - 🚀 **Nexora AI (Crystal Tech Station)**
     - 🌾 **CropIndia AI (Terraced Farm)**
     - 🎓 **Education (Academics)**
     - 📬 **Contact (Roadside Lounge)**

5. **Procedural Web Audio Engine**
   - Native Web Audio API ambient drone and UI sounds.
   - Zero external audio files required. Autoplay policy compliant.

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Browser Only)

1. Extract `murari-3d-portfolio.zip`.
2. Go to [github.com](https://github.com) and create a **Public** repository named `murari-3d-portfolio`.
3. Click **Add file** &rarr; **Upload files**.
4. Drag and drop `index.html`, `style.css`, `main.js`, and `README.md` directly into GitHub.
5. Click **Commit changes**.
6. In **Settings** &rarr; **Pages**, choose branch **`main`** and folder **`/(root)`**, then click **Save**.
7. In 1–2 minutes, your website is live at:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/murari-3d-portfolio/`
